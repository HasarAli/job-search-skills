# Country Conventions — resume/CV norms by market

Lookup table keyed by the country in `goals/search-filters.md`. Norms drift and vary by employer; anything marked **verify locally** should be confirmed with a local source (recruiter, board guidance, recent hires) before relying on it.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| US | Resume | No — can trigger bias screening | No (no DOB, marital status, nationality) | 1 page (<10 yrs exp), 2 max | Letter | MM/YYYY or Month YYYY | Optional; expected for senior/mission-driven roles | LinkedIn |
| Canada | Resume | No | No | 1–2 pages | Letter | Month YYYY | Often expected, keep short | LinkedIn |
| UK | CV (means resume-style doc) | No | No DOB/photo since Equality Act norms | 2 pages standard | A4 | MM/YYYY | Expected for most professional roles | LinkedIn |
| Germany / DACH | Lebenslauf (tabular CV) | Traditionally yes, professional headshot — declining but still common (**verify locally** per employer) | DOB, birthplace, nationality still commonly listed (**verify locally**) | 1–2 pages CV + full "Bewerbungsmappe" for conservative employers | A4 | DD.MM.YYYY | Anschreiben expected, formal register | LinkedIn + Xing (Xing declining; **verify locally** by industry) |
| France | CV | Photo common but optional (**verify locally**) | Age/DOB sometimes listed; nationality rarely | 1 page strongly preferred, 2 for senior | A4 | MM/YYYY | Lettre de motivation expected, formal | LinkedIn; APEC for cadres |
| Nordics (SE/NO/DK/FI) | CV | Photo common and accepted, not required | DOB common, low-drama norms | 1–2 pages | A4 | YYYY-MM (SE) / DD.MM.YYYY — **verify locally** per country | Personal letter ("personligt brev") customary, more personality than UK/US | LinkedIn; Finn.no (NO), TheHub (startups) |
| Japan | Rirekisho (fixed template) + shokumukeirekisho (work history) | Yes on rirekisho, formal photo, strict conventions | DOB, sometimes gender; handwritten rirekisho now rare (**verify locally** for traditional firms) | Rirekisho 1–2 pages fixed form; shokumukeirekisho 1–2 pages | A4/B5 | YYYY年MM月 (era years at traditional firms — **verify locally**) | Not Western-style; motivation section inside rirekisho | LinkedIn weak; BizReach, Rikunabi, Doda, Wantedly |
| India | Resume/CV (terms interchangeable) | Usually no for private sector; government forms differ | DOB and sometimes father's name on traditional/government formats (**verify locally**) | 1–2 pages private sector; freshers 1 page | A4 | DD/MM/YYYY | Optional; email body often serves as one | LinkedIn + Naukri |
| Australia / NZ | Resume/CV | No | No | 2–3 pages accepted, longer than US norm | A4 | DD/MM/YYYY | Expected; selection-criteria statements for government roles | LinkedIn + Seek |

| Brazil | Currículo | Optional, fairly common (**verify locally**) | DOB, marital status traditionally listed, declining | 1–2 pages | A4 | DD/MM/YYYY | Optional; short intro email more common | LinkedIn (very strong) + Gupy, Catho |

## Application notes

- **CV vs resume**: "CV" means a 2-page resume in UK/EU/Commonwealth usage, but a full academic publication record in US/Canada academia. Disambiguate using both country and industry (see `industry-conventions.md`).
- **Anti-discrimination direction of travel**: photo/DOB/marital-status norms are eroding almost everywhere; when in doubt in a Western market, omit. In markets where inclusion is still expected (DACH traditional employers, Japan rirekisho, Gulf), omitting can read as an error — hence **verify locally**.
- **Paper size** matters for rendered PDFs: Letter in US/Canada, A4 nearly everywhere else. Wrong size prints badly and signals a foreign applicant.
- **Language**: apply in the posting's language unless it explicitly invites English. DACH/France/Japan/Brazil postings in the local language expect local-language documents.
- **References**: "References available on request" is dead weight in US/UK; referee names/contacts are sometimes expected up front in AU/NZ and academic contexts (**verify locally**).
