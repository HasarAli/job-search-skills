# Question Bank — facts and history

One question per message, wait for the answer, probe once, move on. What a document in `drop/` already answered, ask as a confirmation: "your 2021 resume says you led a team of 4 at Acme — still accurate?" On a refresh, ask only what the profile is missing or what a new source contradicts.

Achievements and their numbers belong to `highlights`; ask here only for what a role *was*, not for its wins.

## Identity

- Name as it should appear on documents, and any name a document shows differently?
- Where do you live — city and country?
- Work-authorization status where you live?
- Contact details for applications: email, phone, profile URL?

## Timeline — per role, most recent first

- Title, employer, location, start and end dates?
- What did the team do, in one sentence, and what were you responsible for day to day?
- How big was the team, and did anyone report to you?
- What changed because you were there? What would have gone worse without you?
- Why did you leave (or why are you leaving)?

Cover every role back to the start of the career the user wants to be hired for. Earlier roles get one line each — title, employer, dates.

## Skills and credentials

- Which skills and tools do you want to be hired for?
- Which do you have and want to stop using?
- Certifications, licenses, degrees — issuer and year?
- Languages spoken, and at what level?
- Anything you built or published outside a job — open source, writing, talks, side projects?

## Gaps and explanations

- Any employment gap, career change, or short tenure to have a ready explanation for?
- How do you want to explain it? (Their wording is the record — write it down as they say it.)
- Anything in your history you would rather a recruiter hear from you first?

## Interview stories

Two or three, each one a situation the user can tell in two minutes:

- A conflict — with a colleague, a manager, or a stakeholder — and how it ended?
- A failure, and what you changed afterwards?
- A time you led without the authority to, or carried something nobody owned?
- The hardest technical or professional problem you have solved?

Capture the story as they tell it, in `career/career-diary.md`, and the one-line handle in `career/profile.md`.
