# Interview Guide — question banks per stage

Rules that apply to every stage:

- One question at a time, in the main session. Wait for the answer.
- Accept the user's numbers immediately — write them in as given, never demand justification or evidence. If a bullet has no number yet, ask once ("any number you can attach — %, time saved, users, revenue?"); "no number" is an acceptable answer.
- Skip questions already answered by parsed documents; confirm instead ("Your 2021 resume says you led a team of 4 at Acme — still accurate?").
- Short answers are fine. Probe once for depth, then move on.

## Stage 1 — setup

- Which country (or countries) are you job-searching in?
- What industry / field? (One primary; secondaries welcome.)
- What language will your applications be in? Is it different from your working language?
- Which platforms or job boards do you already use or expect to use? Any you refuse to use?
- Are you employed right now? Does the search need to be confidential?
- Tracking defaults in `search-config.md`: follow up after 14 days with no response, retro every 50 applications — keep those or adjust?

## Stage 2 — context

### Timeline walkthrough (per role, most recent first)

- Title, employer, location, start/end dates?
- What did the team/organization do, in one sentence?
- What were you responsible for day to day?
- What changed because you were there? What would have gone worse without you?
- Why did you leave (or why are you leaving)?

### Achievement mining → XYZ bullets

Target format: **"Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]."**

- What's the piece of work from this role you're proudest of?
- What did it improve — and is there any number attached (%, time, money, volume, people, quality metric)?
- How did you do it — what was the method, tool, or decision that made it work?
- Did anyone recognize it (promotion, award, review quote, customer feedback)?
- Any hard problem you fixed under pressure? Any process you created that outlived you?
- Repeat until 2–4 candidate bullets per significant role.

### Skills, stories, gaps

- Core skills/tools you want to be hired for? Skills you have but want to stop using?
- Certifications, licenses, degrees, languages spoken?
- Any employment gaps or career changes we should have a ready explanation for? (Capture the user's framing verbatim — it's their story.)
- Two or three stories you'd want to tell in an interview (conflict, failure, leadership)?

## Stage 3 — targets

- What job titles are you targeting? (List all; rank if possible.)
- For each: what's the one-line pitch — the positioning anchor — for why you fit it?
- What seniority level are you aiming at? Would you go one level down for the right situation?
- What roles, title patterns, or work styles should we **never** pursue, even if they match on paper? (→ "Do not pursue" section.)
- Is there a stretch role you'd want to grow into that we should keep on the radar?

## Stage 4 — filters

### Compensation

- What's your walk-away floor (total comp)? What's your target?
- Currency and pay cadence conventions in your market — annual, monthly, hourly?
- Non-salary must-haves (equity, pension, healthcare, bonus)?

### Location / remote

- Where do you live, and how far will you commute?
- Remote, hybrid, or on-site — what's acceptable, what's preferred?
- Would you relocate? To where, and under what conditions?

### Company type

- Size range (startup / scaleup / enterprise / public sector / nonprofit)?
- Industries or company types to exclude outright?
- Anything about culture or working model that's a hard filter (e.g. on-call, shift work)?

### Logistics & authorization

- Earliest start date? Notice period?
- Travel tolerance (% or days/month)?
- Work authorization status in the target country — do you need visa sponsorship? Any countries where you're authorized already?
- Hours constraints (part-time, caregiving windows, time zones)?
